Omani cultural notes are organised into food, architecture, traditions, and visitor etiquette.

01 / FOOD & GATHERING

Traditional
Omani food.

Omani dishes include shuwa, slow-cooked meat prepared for gatherings, and majboos, spiced rice commonly served with meat. Coffee and dates are associated with hospitality.

Omani halwa with saffron.

02 / PLACE & CRAFT

Omani
architecture.

Historic forts, traditional houses, and contemporary public buildings reflect different periods of Omani design. Muttrah's old quarter is known for traditional buildings and wooden balconies.

03 / TRADITION & EXPERIENCE

Traditions &
experiences.

Markets, craft workshops, frankincense, coffee, dates, and halwa are among the cultural subjects featured in Oman's tourism information.

VISITOR ETIQUETTE

Visitor
etiquette.

Follow posted rules at religious and heritage sites, dress modestly when visiting religious places, and ask before photographing people. Check each venue's current visitor guidance.
