01 / MUSCAT GOVERNORATE

Muscat

Capital on the Gulf of Oman, with a historic harbour quarter, national collections, and landmark architecture.

Top spots & visitor details

  • Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque ↗Sat–Thu, 8–11 am for non-Muslim visitors. OMR 8 admission is listed by Experience Oman; check before visiting.
  • Muttrah Fort ↗Daily, 8 am–11 pm. Listed fee: OMR 3 non-resident adult; OMR 1 resident adult.
  • Royal Opera House Muscat ↗House tours Sat–Thu, 8:30 am–5:30 pm; adult tickets listed from OMR 3.15. Auditorium access depends on availability.
  • National Museum ↗Sat–Thu, 10 am–5 pm; Fri, 2–6 pm. Tourist admission OMR 5; resident OMR 2.

02 / AD DAKHILIYAH

Nizwa

Historic inland city in Ad Dakhiliyah, known for its fort, traditional souq, and Friday livestock market.

Top spots & visitor details

  • Nizwa Fort ↗Sat–Thu, 8 am–8 pm; Fri, 8–11:30 am and 1:30–8 pm. Tourist ticket listings start at OMR 5; confirm at the site.
  • Nizwa Souq ↗Beside the fort. Market and shop hours vary; Friday morning is the main livestock-market period. No general entry ticket listed.
  • Oman Across Ages Museum ↗In nearby Manah. Check the museum’s current hours and ticket rates before travelling.
Add Nizwa to your trip ↗
03 / ASH SHARQIYAH SOUTH

Wadi Shab

Coastal valley with a walking trail, limestone cliffs, swimming pools, and a cave waterfall reached by swimming.

Top spots & visitor details

  • Trail entrance & boat crossing ↗A small local boat crosses the inlet; recent visitor guides list about OMR 1 return. Confirm the price and service at the landing.
  • Wadi pools & cave ↗Open-air trail; no official fixed hours or entry price published on the tourism listing. Start in daylight and allow several hours.
  • Bimmah Sinkhole ↗Nearby coastal stop. Check local access notices and conditions on the day.
Add Wadi Shab to your trip ↗
04 / ASH SHARQIYAH NORTH

Wahiba Sands

Known officially as A’Sharqiyah Sands; Bidiyah is a gateway to desert activities and camps.

Top spots

  • Bidiyah ↗Gateway town and meeting point for many desert trips.
  • Dune drive & sandboarding ↗Operator prices vary; arrange a suitable vehicle and guide before entering the dunes.
  • Desert camps ↗Rates and check-in times are set by each camp; booking is required for overnight stays.
05 / AD DAKHILIYAH

Jebel Akhdar

Mountain plateau with terraced orchards, villages, and hiking routes in the Al Hajar range. Viewpoint hours vary; reserve permits and fees depend on visitor category.

Top spots

  • Saiq Plateau ↗Mountain access and trail visits depend on current conditions; reserve entry permits may apply.
  • Diana’s Point ↗Viewpoint; check site access and any resort visitor rules.
  • Wadi Bani Habib ↗Mountain village and walking area. Gov.om lists reserve permit fees by visitor category; check the official permit page.
  • Permit & current fees ↗Official service lists OMR 1 for adult citizens and OMR 3 for adult residents; a tourist rate is not shown on the service page. Check your category before visiting.
06 / DHOFAR

Salalah

Southern Omani city and base for coastal scenery, springs, frankincense heritage, and khareef landscapes.

Top spots

  • Wadi Darbat ↗Waterfall, lakes and picnic landscape, especially during khareef. The tourism listing gives no fixed entry fee/hours.
  • Al Mughsail Beach & Marneef Cave ↗Coastline and blowholes west of Salalah. Public outdoor site; no standard ticket or schedule listed.
  • Al Baleed Archaeological Park ↗Frankincense Land heritage site; museum hours and ticket rates should be confirmed with the venue.
Visitor details checked 1 November 2026. Venue schedules and rates can change; confirm before travel.
